# Lift Log design brief

## Chosen direction: Flow State

### Design movement
A **soft performance interface**: editorial clarity meets modern training utility. It is less like a spreadsheet or old fitness dashboard, and more like a calm, tactile companion that makes the next set obvious.

### Core principles
- **One clear next action:** Every important screen leads with the one thing to do now; supporting actions are quieter or grouped under tools.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Workout details, planning math, safety notes, import options, and destructive actions stay available without competing for attention.
- **Touch-first confidence:** Large input targets, short labels, and compact quick-fill controls reduce mental load between sets.
- **Meaningful motion:** Press feedback and modal transitions are quick and quiet; no animation delays entry or obscures data.

### Color philosophy
- **Deep graphite (`#14201A`)** anchors primary actions and creates a premium, focused contrast.
- **Soft mineral (`#F4F7F3`)** gives the app an airy canvas without a stark clinical white.
- **Warm white (`#FFFFFF`)** separates interactive surfaces cleanly.
- **Signal lime (`#C8FF66`)** is reserved for momentum, completion, and high-value cues—not every control.
- **Moss (`#365D1B`)** supports success states and secondary emphasis.
- **Coral (`#D65745`)** is used only for destructive or attention-required states.

### Layout paradigm
A portrait-first **focal-card flow**: a concise page header, a single contextual hero/action surface, then a stack of calm, purpose-specific surfaces. Cards have generous rounded corners and breathing room. Navigation is a persistent floating dock instead of a rigid edge-to-edge bar. Wide Web layouts stay centered and phone-shaped rather than expanding into a dashboard.

### Signature elements
- A soft floating navigation dock with a dark active destination.
- Deep-graphite focal surfaces with concise lime or white action controls.
- Compact, dark-selected chips for weeks, percentages, and quick options.
- Clean, high-contrast set-entry fields with a clear visual rhythm.

### Interaction philosophy
The app never asks a person to decipher a dashboard while training. A planned workout, a solo log, a suggested load, and a completed set should each require one obvious tap. Secondary setup and management actions are still easy to find but do not overwhelm the primary moment.

### Animation
- Pressed controls gently compress and fade, then return immediately.
- Sheets and dialogs use fast native fade/slide transitions.
- Completion and selection use color/state change rather than decorative movement.

### Typography system
Use the platform system typeface with a compact editorial hierarchy: small letter-spaced context labels; strong, tightly tracked page titles; and tabular, high-contrast performance numbers. Keep supporting copy short and generously spaced.

### Brand essence and voice
**A clear, capable training log that keeps attention on the workout—not the app.**

Personality: **calm, capable, precise.**

Microcopy examples:
- “Your next session is ready.”
- “Use this as a starting load—then log what you actually did.”

### Wordmark and logo
Retain the existing approved Lift Log app icon and `LL` identity assets without generating or substituting an approximation. In-product use stays typographic and restrained: “Lift Log” appears only where a brand cue is useful, never as decorative filler.

### Signature brand color
Signal lime `#C8FF66` is Lift Log’s ownable momentum color. It marks completed work, selected states, and moments of forward movement while graphite remains the primary action anchor.
